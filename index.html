How is the comparison calculated?

For each fuel: annual kWh × unit rate ÷ 100 + 365 × daily standing charge ÷ 100. We add the fuel costs and compare both tariffs using identical usage. Monthly usage is multiplied by 12; weekly and daily usage are scaled to 365 days. This calculator supports single-rate tariffs; multi-rate tariffs need separate peak and off-peak calculations.
